About Me

I am a Computer Science B.Sc. graduate from Islamic Azad University, Science and Research Branch , with a strong focus on Machine Learning, industrial automation, and EV drive systems.

I have hands-on experience with e-motor production lines and high-voltage battery (HVB) in projects related to brands like Mercedes-Benz and Audi. My work includes station analysis, defining functional sequences for assembly and testing stations, and creating clear technical documentation for rotor, stator, and ZSB lines.

In parallel, I have worked with SolarWatt-based solar panel production processes, documenting and analyzing stages such as stringing, lamination, EL-testing, framing, and final assembly. This combination of software skills, ML knowledge, and industrial experience has shaped my interest in using data-driven methods to improve production quality and efficiency in EV and renewable-energy manufacturing.
